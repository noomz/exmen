<objective>
Extend `HookParser` with a streaming, single-line entry point `parseLine(_:)` returning a typed `HookLineEvent`, adding the two new keys this phase needs: `subtask` (carrying a raw TOML inline table for dynamic spawn, D-05) and `progress` (an integer clamped to 0–100, D-12). Output: `HookParser.parseLine` + `HookLineEvent` enum, exercised by the parser portion of `DynamicSpawnTests`.
</objective>
